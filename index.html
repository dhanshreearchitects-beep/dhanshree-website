The Studio

Architecture that is thoughtful, practical and personal.

Dhanshree Architects is an architecture and interior design studio based in Meerut, Uttar Pradesh.

We work across residential, commercial, workspace, retail and renovation projects with a focus on functional planning, contemporary aesthetics, practical material selection and close coordination throughout the design process.

Our work extends across Meerut, Modipuram, Noida, Delhi NCR, Ghaziabad, Muradnagar, Muzaffarnagar, Bijnor and nearby regions.

What We Do

Design services from
concept to completion.

Whether it is a new home, farmhouse, office, commercial project or renovation, our process combines planning, visualisation, detailing and site execution.

01

Architecture

Site planning, floor planning, façade design, working drawings, elevation development and architectural visualisation.

02

Interior Design

Space planning, furniture layouts, material selection, lighting, ceilings, joinery and complete interior detailing.

03

Commercial Design

Offices, studios, showrooms, retail environments, professional workplaces and commercial spaces.

04

Turnkey Execution

Design, vendor coordination, site supervision, material execution and project delivery from concept to handover.

05

Renovation

Residential renovation, office transformation, façade redesign and complete interior upgrades.

06

Vastu Planning

Vastu-conscious planning thoughtfully integrated with contemporary architecture and functional space planning.
